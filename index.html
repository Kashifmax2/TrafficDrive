Frequently Asked Questions
How does organic search traffic work?▼
The bot searches for your keywords on search engines, finds your website in results, and clicks through to visit it naturally.
Do I need proxies?▼
Yes, proxies are recommended to simulate visits from different locations and avoid detection. Use the Proxies page to import and validate them.
What is the Scroll System?▼
The Scroll System simulates human-like scrolling behavior on visited pages, making traffic appear more natural and increasing dwell time.
How many threads should I use?▼
Start with 5 threads. You can increase up to 20 depending on your system resources and proxy pool size.
